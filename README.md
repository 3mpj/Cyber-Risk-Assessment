## CyberIQ 
### A risk assessment and management platform using National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cyber Security Framework (CSF)

Organizations depend on information systems to carry out their day to day business
functions. The success of these functions depends on protecting the confidentiality,
integrity, availability of information processed, stored and transmitted by those systems
and the privacy of individuals.

Threats to information systems vary and successful attacks can result in serious and/or
catastrophic damage to organizational operations, assets, individuals, other
organizations and the Nation. Therefore, it is important that all different level
stakeholders throughout the organization understand their responsibilities and are
accountable for protecting organizational assets and for managing cybersecurity
risk.

Cyber Risk Management, also called cybersecurity risk management, is the process of
identifying, prioritizing, managing and monitoring risks to information systems.

### Problem and Solution
The main elements of Cybersecurity are; People, Technology and Process.

People often represent the weakest link in the security chain and are chronically
responsible for failure of security systems.

With many systems already in place to tackle risk assessment and management, many
of them being freemium services are based on scanning the computer systems
operating system key directories and other disk directories for signs of vulnerability or
compromise. These systems do their screening and scanning irregardless of whether
the end user knows or doesn’t know about cybersecurity risks & management and
frameworks that build governance and compliance for cybersecurity preparedness in an
organization or even as a day to day internet user.

CyberIQ aims to bridge the weak link between the people and the other
elements of cybersecurity by ensuring proper education(management) to enable the
users to know cybersecurity 101 and cybersecurity risk scenarios for individuals, table
top exercises to simulate different cybersecurity scenarios and an assessment based
on NIST CyberSecurity Framework to help in risk assessment and management for
organizations.

**Outlines the functions; Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover**
